Our mission

Make finding a willing donor a calm, clear process.

In an emergency, families often rely on scattered phone lists and social media posts. Blood Connect replaces that uncertainty with a structured, verified directory and a traceable request workflow.

Donors decide when they are available. Receivers search by blood group and location, then send a request with the hospital and urgency details. Administrators verify donors, review reports and keep the platform accountable. Every role has a separate dashboard and only sees what it needs.

Donor eligibility

A quick safety check before you donate.

Blood Connect applies basic eligibility rules to every profile. Final medical screening always happens at the donation centre — these checks simply help donors and receivers make informed choices.

Health comes first.

If you are unwell, pregnant, recently had surgery or a tattoo, or take certain medication, speak to a clinician before donating.

Age 18–65

Valid identification may be required at the centre.

At least 50 kg

Donors must meet the minimum weight requirement.

90 days since last donation

The platform calculates your next eligible date automatically.

Feeling well

Hydrated, rested and free of infection on donation day.

Recent illness or infection

Wait until you have fully recovered and a clinician approves.

Pregnancy or recent birth

Follow your doctor's guidance on when it is safe to donate.

{{ section_heading("Trust & safety", "Practical safeguards, built into the workflow") }}

Admin verification

New donor profiles are reviewed before they can accept requests. Verified badges are visible across search.

Consent-first contact

Donors choose whether to share their phone number. Receivers see it only after an accepted request.

Report & review

Any signed-in user can report suspicious behaviour. Administrators review and document every report.

Secure by default

Hashed passwords, CSRF protection, role-based route guards and input validation on the server.

In a life-threatening emergency

Call your local emergency services and contact the hospital blood bank first. Blood Connect helps coordinate willing donors but is not an ambulance or emergency dispatch service.

Privacy & consent

Donor phone numbers are never shown in public search results. Receivers must be signed in, have an accepted request, and the donor must have contact sharing enabled. Administrators can access contact details only for moderation and support.
